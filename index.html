How do staff submit tech issues?

This is a policy that can be catered to your school’s needs. Staff may submit tickets directly, or you can coordinate with your school's Tech Liaison. Here is guidance for submitting a ticket:

  1. Use this link and this link only to submit tech issues. Suggest staff bookmark it for ease of use.
  2. Submit information following the WHO, WHAT, WHERE, and sometimes WHY format:
    • Who: Who is having the issue (local to one person or multiple)? CC those affected on the ticket so I can follow up.
    • What: Clearly describe what is happening. Use direct language to help guide me through the problem.
    • Where: Where is the issue occurring (device, location, classroom, etc.)?
    • Why: Sometimes useful—explain the ask or problem to help me understand how to solve it.
  3. Include any specific information you think is relevant (error messages, screenshots, steps that lead to the issue).
  4. Check for responses in your inbox; I may have follow-up questions to help resolve the issue.
  5. If a ticket is closed due to no response, it can be automatically re-opened by replying to the ticket email.
  6. If you think it could be a ticket, it probably should be.
